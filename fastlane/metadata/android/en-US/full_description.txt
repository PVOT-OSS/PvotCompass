Compass is a simple, precise compass with a clean black dial and nothing in the way.

Point your phone and read your heading at a glance: a large degree readout with the direction (N, NE, E, …), a smooth rotating dial with a mark every 2° and a red north arrow. A gentle haptic tick every 30° lets you feel the turn without looking.

Where you are
• Your coordinates in degrees, minutes and seconds
• The name of the place you are in
• Your elevation above sea level

Built for every screen
• Works in portrait and landscape, with the heading corrected for how you hold the device
• Layouts for phones, tablets and foldables

Home screen widget
• The compass dial with your last known location, one tap away from the full app
• Updates only when you open the app, so it costs no battery

Private and open
• No ads, no accounts, no tracking
• Location is used only to show your coordinates, place and elevation; the compass works without it
• Free and open source under the GPL-3.0-or-later licence: github.com/PVOT-OSS/PvotCompass

Compass shows magnetic north. Keep the phone away from magnets and metal, and move it in a figure eight if the heading seems off.
